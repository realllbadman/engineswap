Our Story

From San Antonio to Nationwide

EngineSwap Autos started in 2010 with a simple idea: give builders, collectors, and enthusiasts access to quality engines and classic cars at honest prices. What began as a small operation in San Antonio has grown into one of the most trusted names in LS swaps and classic car sales in the country.

Over 15 years, we have helped thousands of customers find the right engine for their build, the right classic for their garage, or the right complete swap package to make their project a reality. Every unit we sell is compression tested and backed by our 1-year unlimited mileage warranty.

Our connections with salvage yards, GM dealers, and private collections across North America allow us to source low-mileage truck LS pullouts, rare muscle cars, and complete swap packages that you simply will not find anywhere else at these prices.
